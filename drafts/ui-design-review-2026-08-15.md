# Co-Exist UI Design Review

_EcodiaOS design-critique workflow, 15 Aug 2026. 8 eyes-on cluster critics + synthesis, run against authed screenshots of every live admin page + key member pages, scored on the craft arsenal (DESIGN.md / next-level-craft.md) and the app own DESIGN-SYSTEM.md._

## Executive verdict

Co-Exist is not suffering from generic-AI slop  -  the Law 0 floor is genuinely clean (no gradient blobs, no eyebrow chips, no dark-glow, no fabricated logos) and the photography-forward hero work is premium enough to stand next to any friend's app. What drags it below par is a single root pattern that recurs in seven of eight clusters: the austerity direction never designed the ABSENCE states. Every surface bets on a real photograph or a big number, and the moment the photo is missing/unloaded or the dataset is empty, the screen collapses to grey wireframe rectangles, black avatar voids, dashed-box "broken" SVGs, all-zero stat rows, and truncated labels  -  so on a real page where 30-40% of tiles are in that state, the imagery-first system inverts into looking unfinished. The secondary pattern is that blanket rules ("cards white period", "shadow-sm only", "colour on tiny badges only", "numbers alone") stripped the compensating craft that dense DATA surfaces need  -  and stripped craft the codebase ALREADY has (micro-viz.tsx built and unused, the stat-card theme prop wired then ignored, activity colours defined then suppressed). The through-line of the fix is not decoration: it is to design the empty/absent/few states the photography-first bet depends on, and to spend the freed craft budget on the data surfaces (committed ground, tinted shadow, per-metric accent, the micro-viz you already built). The proof the direction over-corrected is that the insights page  -  the one surface that quietly relaxes the rules with green charts and coloured tags  -  is the most premium screen in the app.

## Did over-direction cost quality? (direct answer)

Yes  -  you over-directed, and it cost real quality, but on exactly one axis and the DESIGN-SYSTEM.md file is the literal record of it. Where restraint was RIGHT and must not be touched: the full-bleed photography heroes, the serif CO-EXIST wordmark, the torn-paper/wave divider signature, numbers-as-hero typography, the disciplined single-green accent, and the clean Law 0 floor. Do not respond to this by adding gradient blobs, floating rings or eyebrow chips  -  that ban is correct. Austerity became DAMAGE in two specific places. First and worst: the direction never wrote a designed state for absence, so 'photography-forward' with no fallback means a missing image renders as a grey void (event grids, Photos page), a black disc (profile avatar), or a generic green gradient that is itself the Law-0 slop tell it was meant to avoid  -  this is the single biggest 'not up to par' driver. Second: four blanket rules ('cards are white, period', 'shadow-sm only', 'colour only on tiny badges', 'numbers are the hero' read as 'numbers alone') stripped the figure/ground and scannability tools a dense data surface needs, leaving the admin dashboard's five stat cards reading as invisible wireframe tiles next to an insights page that looks premium precisely because it broke those rules. The tell that you over-corrected rather than under-built: the craft is already in the codebase (MiniBar/MiniSparkline/MiniRing sit unused, the BentoStatCard theme prop is ignored, the activity colour palette is suppressed), so the fix is to STOP forbidding craft you already built and to design the empty state, not to sprinkle decoration back in. 'Less is more' held; the '10% finish' and the absence states did not.

## Prioritised punch-list

### 1. Design the image-absent state once, propagate everywhere  
**P0 - M effort - high impact**  
**Where:** OptimizedImage / Card.Overlay  -  admin dashboard Upcoming Registrations, admin-events, admin-collectives, admin-photos, member home cards, profile avatar  
**Problem:** Missing or not-yet-loaded covers collapse to flat grey/taupe rectangles (dashboard, events, collectives, photos), a solid black disc (profile avatar), or a generic green gradient with a faint watermark  -  the same generic-AI gradient-where-a-photo-belongs tell the austerity was meant to avoid. On a real page 30-40% of tiles are in this state, so the whole surface reads as a broken wireframe. This is the #1 root cause of 'not up to par'.  
**Fix:** Two-part fix in one shared component. (1) Reserve aspect ratio and paint a brand-tinted skeleton (primary-50/moss-50 shimmer) behind the image wrapper with an inline data-URI blurDataURL so a card NEVER shows raw neutral grey or page-white; fade the img in on onLoad. (2) On genuine null/error, render a per-card editorial tile: activity-colour ground keyed off activity_type (sprout/sky/bark/plum/coral/moss, not one green), the activity watermark at ~18-24%, and the event date/initial set LARGE as a typographic hero. Move the fallback onto an onError handler. Reuse the leaderboard's initials-in-tinted-circle for the avatar  -  that fallback already exists two screens away.

### 2. Replace broken-looking empty-state illustrations and suppress all-zero stat rows  
**P0 - S effort - high impact**  
**Where:** EmptyState presetIllustrations (admin-tasks, admin-updates); AdminHeroStatRow zero-case (admin-updates, admin-applications)  
**Problem:** Empty states center a thin grey dashed-rectangle SVG with a dot  -  the universal visual language for 'image failed to load'  -  as the largest graphic on a healthy screen. Separately, admin-updates renders four white cards each showing '0' directly above a 'No updates found' empty state: two 'nothing here' signals stacked, reading as a data-load failure.  
**Fix:** Swap the dashed-box preset for a real branded mark (a large Megaphone/ClipboardList Lucide watermark at ~15-20% in a primary-50 rounded tile, or a real org photo). When the primary metric is 0, suppress the hero stat row entirely and let the well-written empty-state copy carry the screen. Design zero/one/few/many explicitly.

### 3. Fix truncated labels and headings that read as broken layout  
**P0 - S effort - high impact**  
**Where:** AdminHeroStat label ('CAMPAIGNS SE...' on admin-email); home section heading ('COMING UP IN YOUR COLL...')  
**Problem:** A headline stat label clips mid-word ('Campaigns Sent' -> 'CAMPAIGNS SE...') and the home section heading truncates mid-word ('...YOUR COLL...'). A truncated label on a hero element instantly reads as unpolished to any cold-start viewer.  
**Fix:** Drop the single-line truncate class; widen the stat grid (AdminHeroStatRow max-w is too tight for four 2-word labels) or allow 2-line labels with leading-tight; on the home heading render it at the tiny journal-label scale the DS specifies and allow text-wrap:balance to two lines. One heading system per screen.

### 4. Stop leaking raw JSON blobs and UUIDs to the audit surface  
**P0 - M effort - high impact**  
**Where:** admin-audit-log (audit-log.tsx detail + target lines)  
**Problem:** Every audit row prints raw stringified JSON ({"new_role":"leader"}) as its description and full 36-char UUIDs as the target ('collective: 14c3f087-...'). It is a developer debug console, not a product surface, and it is unusable  -  an admin cannot tell which collective that is.  
**Fix:** Add a formatDetails(action, payload) mapper turning each action into a human sentence ('Changed role to Leader', 'Updated name, region, timezone'). Resolve target_id to the entity display name via the join already used for profiles; where a name genuinely cannot resolve, show a short 8-char mono token with copy-on-click, never the full UUID.

### 5. Re-commit the stat cards: per-metric accent + tinted ground + wire the built micro-viz  
**P1 - M effort - high impact**  
**Where:** BentoStatCard / AdminHeroStat  -  admin dashboard, admin-events, admin-photos, admin-insights attendance grid, admin-email/surveys/updates  
**Problem:** KPI cards sit on a barely-visible sage tint (or bare white), no border, invisible shadow-sm on the near-white ground, a ghost icon chip, and zero committed accent  -  they read as invisible wireframe tiles floating strong numbers. The BentoStatCard theme prop is passed but IGNORED, and MiniBar/MiniSparkline/MiniRing are built in micro-viz.tsx and sit unused, so the dashboard looks a full tier below the insights page.  
**Fix:** Keep numbers-as-hero and white-ish cards, but restore three things the rules stripped: (1) re-wire the ignored theme/accent so MEMBERS/COLLECTIVES/EVENTS/ATTENDEES/HOURS each carry a scannable coloured icon badge; (2) add a surface-tinted shadow (not grey) and a slim accent rail so cards separate from the ground; (3) wire the existing MiniSparkline under trended metrics (member growth) and MiniRing on % metrics (return rate, sign-in rate). Scale the numeral to the DS hero spec.

### 6. Fix inverted hero-number hierarchy  -  promote the actionable/flagship number  
**P1 - S effort - high impact**  
**Where:** admin-contacts (emergency phone), admin-shop (product price), admin-insights (impact numbers)  
**Problem:** The design system's own 'numbers are the hero' rule is inverted where it matters most: on Emergency Contacts the phone number  -  the one datum you dial in an emergency  -  is text-xs neutral-400, fainter than the name; in the shop the product name is huge-bold while the price (the declared hero number) is small grey; on insights the flagship 66,493 trees / 6,680kg rubbish get no more weight than an admin sign-in count.  
**Fix:** Invert the weight assignments. Promote phone to text-base font-semibold neutral-900 tabular-nums and demote the note; promote price to text-lg/xl bold tabular-nums and calm the product title; give the two impact metrics a dedicated text-4xl/5xl treatment in the sanctioned moss-tinted impact band with the tree/trash icon at scale.

### 7. Make the scarce colour carry information instead of washing to invisibility  
**P1 - M effort - high impact**  
**Where:** admin-users (role badges), admin-contacts (category tints), admin-surveys (activity pills), system-wide  
**Problem:** Colour is rationed so hard it never registers as the olive-green brand until you reach the insights charts, AND where it survives it does no work: every user row shows the same washed-out sage 'Participant' pill (a monotone wall that also fails AA), every survey row is identical green-on-green suppressing the defined activity palette, and category colour-coding is near-white and illegible.  
**Fix:** Suppress the badge entirely for the default 'participant' role and show a higher-contrast pill (*-200/*-800) ONLY for elevated/exception states (leader/admin/suspended). Colour activity/'Impact -' pills by activity_type using the existing sprout/sky/bark/plum/coral/moss tokens. Strengthen category tints to *-100 bg / *-600 icon plus a slim category-coloured accent bar on group headers. Colour then instantly flags the rows that matter.

### 8. Fill or shrink the empty hero bands so the strongest asset earns its height  
**P1 - S effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** admin-collectives, admin-users, admin-shop, admin-legal, admin-tasks/surveys/updates  
**Problem:** The committed olive hero is the best craft in the app, but it is left mostly empty on many pages  -  Collectives is a tall band with only a title where the identical Events band carries a stat row; Users, Shop and Legal are big empty sage voids; Workflows holds only a title plus one ghost button. A big block of committed brand colour with nothing in it reads as unfinished, and the divergence between full and empty siblings reads as incoherence.  
**Fix:** Reuse AdminHeroStatRow (already imported in several of these pages) to fill each band with numbers already available client-side (N collectives/members/events; total/participants/staff/suspended; products/in-stock/low-stock/live). Where there is genuinely no stat, shrink the band height and left-align the title into an editorial relationship rather than centring it over a void.

### 9. Finish the utility controls and give the primary CTA a confident fill  
**P1 - M effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** member events filter dropdowns, admin-email AI textarea + 'Draft with AI' button, member profile action row, front-door filter row  
**Problem:** 'Less is more' was applied until controls have no ground, no border and no states: filter dropdowns are flat grey wireframe blocks, the AI prompt textarea is a dead grey void with no placeholder, and the main 'Draft with AI' CTA is desaturated until it reads as disabled  -  while Create Survey/New Update elsewhere are confident dark green. Four identical ghost buttons on the profile give the row no primary and no tactility.  
**Fix:** Give form controls a white ground + border-neutral-200, a chevron/placeholder, and the four-state treatment (rest/hover/focus-visible/active) with a branded active state when a filter is set. Give 'Draft with AI' the same primary dark-green Button variant as the other create actions and reserve muted sage for secondary. Differentiate the profile action row: tint icons primary-600, add press feedback, promote the most-used action to a filled primary.

### 10. Return tactile depth to list rows  -  tinted shadow + hover lift  
**P1 - M effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** admin-users, admin-contacts, admin-applications rows; member leaderboard; system-wide near-white ground  
**Problem:** Rows are white + a barely-visible neutral-100 hairline + flat grey shadow-sm on the surface-1 ground, so adjacent rows barely separate and the whole list reads as a flat spreadsheet dump with no sense that a row is a distinct, clickable object. The leaderboard reads as a stack of equal tiles rather than a ranked ladder.  
**Fix:** Keep the white ground but add one surface-tinted shadow token (e.g. 0 4px 16px rgba(71,79,47,0.08)) plus a hover state that lifts the row (subtle translate-y + deeper tinted shadow), guarded @media (hover:hover). For the leaderboard switch separate cards to one surface with divide-neutral-100 hairlines so it reads as an editorial ladder with the medalled top three as the accent.

### 11. Unify to one primitive per object and reconcile DESIGN-SYSTEM.md to the shipped code  
**P1 - L effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** stat cards (3 systems), list rows (3 languages), hero mastheads (2 type systems), DESIGN-SYSTEM.md vs card.tsx  
**Problem:** The same object is styled multiple ways: three stat-card treatments (documented AdminHeroStat spec, shipped BentoStatCard, Photos StatBox), three list-row languages, two hero title personalities (serif wordmark vs heavy grotesk 'EXPLORE'), and the doc declares rounded-2xl while every card ships rounded-md. The 'source of truth' contradicts the render, so the real bar is undefined and the app reads as several apps.  
**Fix:** Pick one canonical stat card (the tinted bento, once the accent is re-wired), one AdminListRow (the Applications status-strip treatment is the strongest template), and one hero masthead grammar driven by useAdminHeader across every admin route. Unify hero titling to the brand display serif. Ratify one radius (move to rounded-2xl per the doc's own intent for a softer premium read) and rewrite DESIGN-SYSTEM.md to describe what actually ships.

### 12. Design the few-case and vary card weight  -  kill card soup and dead grid space  
**P2 - M effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** admin dashboard (18 identical tiles), admin-events (single-event groups), admin-photos (4-col padding), admin-legal (7 identical cards), admin-audit-log (no date grouping)  
**Problem:** The dominant layout is a symmetric deck of identical tiles with no hierarchy  -  a collective with 52 registrations looks identical to one with 1; single-event groups leave two empty white columns; the photos grid pads short events with empty grey squares; seven identical legal cards make Terms of Service read as important as Cookie Policy; the audit log runs 25 undifferentiated rows with no day separators.  
**Fix:** Introduce weight and design the few-case. Lead with one wide 'next event across the org' feature card above a tighter secondary row; vary the count pill by fill ratio (near-capacity reads hot, 1 reads quiet). Branch grids on item count (1-2 events get a wider hero card or a ghost 'Add event' tile; photos adapt 1/2/3/4-up, never empty grey padding). Replace boxed legal cards with an editorial hairline list grouped Legal vs Informational. Add sticky date dividers and collapse duplicate audit runs into an '×4' count.

### 13. Raise washed-out meta text and faint affordances above the legibility floor  
**P2 - S effort - medium impact**  
**Where:** admin-legal (neutral-300 dates), admin-contacts/events meta on bright covers, member leaderboard rank numbers, legal/shop edit icons  
**Problem:** Austerity pushed secondary text past legible into ghosted: legal 'Updated 6 May 2026' dates are text-neutral-300 and effectively invisible (an AA miss), meta lines wash out on bright cover photos, rank numbers for positions 4+ are near-invisible on white, and primary edit affordances are neutral-300 with sub-44px hit areas.  
**Fix:** Set a contrast floor: secondary/meta text never below neutral-500 for anything a user must read; resting action icons never below neutral-400 with a clear neutral-700 hover; deepen the cover legibility gradient (from-black/80 via-black/40) or add a text drop-shadow token verified against the brightest covers; pad row action controls to 44px.

## Per-cluster verdicts

### front-door (7 findings)
The hero is genuinely premium  -  a full-bleed real team photograph, an elegant serif CO-EXIST AUSTRALIA wordmark with a layered drop-shadow, and a torn-wave divider into white. That is photography-forward restraint done RIGHT and it should be protected. But the moment you scroll past it the page falls off a cliff: the Upcoming Registrations grid is a wall of ~18 near-identical grey gradient rectangles (the covers didn't paint, so the section reads as a broken wireframe), and the five stat cards are so austerity-stripped  -  faint sage tint, no border, no depth, no accent, a barely-visible icon chip  -  that they read as light-grey placeholders on a white page rather than the confident data surface a dashboard needs. This is the classic over-direction failure: the "flat white, shadow-sm only, no coloured backgrounds, no depth" law that makes the HERO sing is the same law that guts the DATA surface, where figure/ground separation and a scannable per-metric accent are the whole job. The quality swings violently between the hero (10/10) and everything below it (4/10).

_Over-direction note:_ The over-direction cost real craft on exactly one axis and helped on another  -  and the founder's instinct is correct. Correct restraint: the hero (photography-forward, serif wordmark, wave divider) and numbers-as-hero typography. Those are premium precisely BECAUSE they are austere; leave them. Where austerity became damage is the DATA surface. Four DESIGN-SYSTEM.md rules  -  'NO coloured card backgrounds, cards are white, period', 'shadow-sm only, no coloured shadows', 'no bg-gradient on cards', and 'no borders on the admin/leader stat cards'  -  collectively stripped every tool a dashboard uses to create figure/ground and scannability: committed ground, tinted shadow, per-metric accent, depth. The result is stat cards that read as invisible and a card grid that collapses to grey when its one crutch (real photography) fails to load. The fix is not to add slop; it is to recognise that 'less is more' is a rule for CONTENT-forward surfaces (hero, event cards with real photos) and the wrong rule for a DENSE DATA surface, where the arsenal's colour+light dimension (scarce committed accent, tinted shadow) is what makes restraint read as premium instead of unfinished. The single highest-leverage move: make covers load with a brand-tinted blur placeholder so the Upcoming grid never shows raw grey, then re-wire the ignored per-metric accent on the stat cards.

### programme (7 findings)
When a real photo is loaded, this cluster is genuinely premium: the collective cards with real group portraits and the mobile Events frame (Biggest-Event 21:9 spotlight, clean stat hero, full-width green CTA) are imagery-first, confident, and correctly restrained. The problem is everything downstream of the happy path. The design bets the entire card on a photograph, but gives absence no designed state: unloaded and missing covers collapse to grey wireframe rectangles or an identical green gradient panel, and on a full page where 30-40% of tiles are in that state the imagery-first system inverts into looking broken. That, not the loaded state, is what reads as "not up to par." Secondary misses are flat numbers-in-boxes stats, undesigned single-card grid rows, and the Collectives hero being empty where the Events hero is invested.

_Over-direction note:_ The austerity is applied correctly on the happy path and wrongly on the edges. Correct restraint: real-photo cards, the mobile hero, the single 21:9 spotlight above an equal grid, the clean segmented toggles - here 'less is more' produces genuine premium and must not be touched. Where over-direction cost craft is entirely in the DEGRADED and EDGE states, because 'no decorative noise' stripped out any intentional treatment for them. Three concrete costs: (1) a photo-less or slow card has no designed ground, so absence collapses to a wireframe rectangle - the single biggest driver of the 'not up to par' read; (2) the one fallback that WAS built is a flat green gradient, which is itself the Law 0 slop tell the austerity was meant to avoid; (3) 'numbers are the hero' was read as 'numbers alone,' so the stats are inert boxes when the micro-viz components to make them live already exist in the system. The fix is not to add slop - it is to extend the same restraint into the states the direction never covered: a designed empty tile, a designed few-case, and a stat that shows a trend.

### people (11 findings)
These three CRUD screens are not sloppy - the Law 0 floor is largely clear (clean white ground, no gradient blobs, no dark-glow, no eyebrow chips, a genuine torn-edge signature on the green band). What makes them read "not up to par" is that the austerity in DESIGN-SYSTEM.md was applied WITHOUT the compensating craft that makes restraint read as intentional rather than unfinished. The rows are flattened to wireframe hairlines with no depth or hierarchy, so they scan as a CSV dump; the one scarce color (role badges) is washed-out sage-on-sage repeated 30 times into a monotone wall; and the layout is a mobile list stretched across a desktop canvas with vast empty gutters. Most tellingly the system's own "numbers are the hero" law is inverted on the Emergency Contacts screen, where the phone number - the single actionable datum in an emergency - is the faintest text on the page. The fix is not decoration; it is restoring hierarchy, tactile depth (tinted shadow + hover), desktop composition, and letting the scarce color do information work.

_Over-direction note:_ The over-direction is real and it IS the cause here - but the lesson is not 'plain is wrong', it is 'plain was shipped without the craft that earns it'. Three self-imposed rules backfired: (1) 'Numbers are the hero' is literally INVERTED on Emergency Contacts - phone numbers render text-xs text-neutral-400, fainter than the name, so the most important datum is the weakest element. (2) 'Less is more / shadow-sm only / no colored shadows' stripped rows to white + neutral-100 hairline + flat grey shadow at space-y-1.5, so nothing reads as a distinct, tappable object - the list looks like a wireframe. (3) 'NO colored card backgrounds, color used sparingly' left the ONE surviving color signal - the role badge - as low-contrast primary-500-on-primary-100, repeated identically down every row into wallpaper. Where restraint is CORRECTLY applied and must be preserved: the white ground itself (clean, not generic), the absence of gradient/glow slop, the torn-edge divider, and the Applications status strip. The correct response is to hold the restraint and return the craft: make the hero datum loud, add tinted-shadow depth + a hover lift, compose the layout for desktop, and make the scarce accent carry information (badge only elevated roles, strengthen category color). Do not add decorative noise.

### insights-media (8 findings)
These two pages are where the over-direction shows most bluntly. Photos is genuinely broken-looking below the fold: the top two event cards prove the design works when real photography loads (full-bleed 4-up strips of real conservation work), but every card beneath collapses into raw grey rectangles because there is no image fallback and the 4-column grid pads short events with empty grey squares.
